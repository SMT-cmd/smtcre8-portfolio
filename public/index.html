02 / THE METHOD

Observe. Think.
Create.

Good products start long before someone opens a code editor. I move from understanding a problem to building, testing and improving an answer.

01

Observe

Study the environment and listen for the friction people live with.

02

Validate

Question assumptions, practical constraints and the business case.

03

Design & build

Plan the experience, develop the system and use the right tools responsibly.

04

Improve

Test what is real, fix what fails and keep refining the solution.
