A PRACTICAL THINKER IN TWO WORLDS

Markets, technology and human-centered problem solving.

As a forex tutor, I focus on helping people understand the mechanics of trading—not selling shortcuts. As a digital innovator, I connect strategy, design, development and responsible automation to build online experiences that solve real problems.

My work moves between software, online business, financial-market education and hands-on experiments. I trade forex, teach what I study, and write trading books while building websites, web applications, useful business tools and trading analysis systems. These are different disciplines, but they come from the same habit: pay attention to a problem, investigate it, and make something practical.

A problem becomes a product

Letterhead Engine began with a request from my brother for an editable letterhead. I chose to build a tool around the need rather than produce a single document. Other projects grew from questions about retail access, fitness consistency, study materials, trading education and interactive communities. I prefer showing the real work—and how it changes—over presenting every idea as a finished business.

Forex tutoring that makes the fundamentals make sense

I teach practical forex concepts including market structure, chart reading, candlesticks, pip values, lot sizing, trading psychology and risk management. Through SLT Trade Hub and its Trading Library, I develop accessible learning materials and original guides such as Synthetic Indices 101. My goal is financial-market literacy and disciplined decision-making, never guaranteed returns.
