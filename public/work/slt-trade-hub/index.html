THE PROJECT TODAY

Public education platform · active product work

The trading library has a public website and dynamic catalogue. Trading bots and broker integrations are separate research and application components; no strategy outcome is guaranteed.

DISCIPLINE

Trading and education

01 / THE STARTING POINT

Why I created it.

Trading education is more useful when learners can revisit material, study structured examples and build responsible habits beyond a one-off signal or lesson.

02 / THE DIRECTION

The design approach.

Bring educational books, live instruction, tools and community learning under a common identity while keeping trader risk, broker integration and educational content distinct.

03 / INSIDE THE PROJECT

What the experience includes.

  • A public Trading Library with individual book pages and reader access flows.
  • Forex and synthetic-index teaching resources, structured guidance and trading psychology material.
  • Trading community and educational programme pages.
  • Separate research and automated-analysis work across supported broker environments.

04 / IMPLEMENTATION

What powers it.

The main site and related systems use modern TypeScript web tooling, auth and data services. Library access and payments are distinct from trading bot execution; live performance claims require independently recorded evidence.

05 / MY CONTRIBUTION

My role.

Forex trader, tutor, author and creator of the educational product ecosystem.
