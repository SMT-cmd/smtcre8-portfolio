THE PROJECT TODAY

Public web project

The repository holds a substantial web storefront. Availability of products, purchases and fulfilment depends on actual business operations.

DISCIPLINE

Retail and commerce

01 / THE STARTING POINT

Why I created it.

Buyers evaluating technology or vehicles need product details and a straightforward way to express interest rather than a scattered collection of offers.

02 / THE DIRECTION

The design approach.

An approachable catalogue with distinct product cards, images and a consistent retail identity for AfriNet Hub.

03 / INSIDE THE PROJECT

What the experience includes.

  • Product browsing across gadgets and premium autos.
  • Product presentation with imagery, detail information and enquiry-oriented actions.
  • Catalogue administration and image workflows represented in the source.

04 / IMPLEMENTATION

What powers it.

A browser-delivered React interface backed by Firebase integration and image-handling utilities. The single-file implementation is a snapshot of the public storefront source, not evidence of fulfilled transactions.

05 / MY CONTRIBUTION

My role.

Commerce concept, digital presentation and interface implementation.
