COMMERCE / PROJECT CASE STUDY

Global Webstores.

A multi-vendor commerce platform that helps independent sellers build their own storefronts while sharing a wider product-discovery marketplace.

Explore product website ↗Request something similar ↗

THE PRODUCT TODAY

Public marketplace · active improvements

The website is publicly accessible. Merchant onboarding, product configuration, payments, delivery and account workflows are being strengthened; the existence of a public site does not mean every checkout path is finished.

DISCIPLINE

Commerce platform

Built around practical needs, with ongoing refinements where needed.

01 / THE STARTING POINT

Why I started it.

Independent sellers need a credible place to present their products, reach buyers and operate a recognizable store without building their own e-commerce infrastructure.

02 / THE IDEA

How the solution took shape.

Global Webstores combines the discoverability of a shared mall with distinct seller-controlled storefronts. The aim is to make a small merchant feel like a real business, not just a listing in someone else's catalogue.

03 / THE EXPERIENCE

What the product includes.

  • A marketplace explorer for finding products and sellers across stores.
  • Merchant registration, dashboard and branded storefront routes, including vendor subdomain work.
  • Product creation with images, configurable variants, prices and store-specific presentation.
  • Buyer account journeys, purchase and payment integrations, fulfilment and vendor operations.

04 / THE BUILD

How it works.

Built as a Next.js platform with a database-backed merchant and product domain, Cloudinary media handling and Paystack payment integrations. Finance, shipping-wallet accounting and validation paths are under active repair and should not be portrayed as completely certified.

05 / MY CONTRIBUTION

The work behind it.

Product vision, system requirements, store experience, implementation direction, technical reviews and iterative QA.
