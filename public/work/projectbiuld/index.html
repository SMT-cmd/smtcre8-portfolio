DISCIPLINE

Academic resources

01 / THE STARTING POINT

Why I created it.

Students researching project topics benefit from a searchable catalogue organised by department and subject rather than hunting through unstructured files.

02 / THE DIRECTION

The design approach.

Build a research-material storefront with clear topic pages, purchasing flow and administrator-managed listings.

03 / INSIDE THE PROJECT

What the experience includes.

  • Searchable academic topics and departments.
  • Project/topic detail pages and material-request workflow.
  • Admin-managed topic, order and content data.
  • Authentication, payment initiation and document asset integrations.

04 / IMPLEMENTATION

What powers it.

Next.js, TypeScript, Supabase, Cloudinary and Paystack are used in the source. The project documentation explicitly calls out deployment credentials and webhook requirements.

05 / MY CONTRIBUTION

My role.

Academic marketplace concept, navigation and application direction.
