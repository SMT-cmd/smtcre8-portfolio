DISCIPLINE

Playful web experiment

01 / THE STARTING POINT

Why I created it.

Not every idea needs to become a company. This began as a humorous concept around a familiar Nigerian food, Akara, with deliberately over-the-top business language.

02 / THE DIRECTION

The design approach.

Build a small branded webpage that feels unexpectedly premium while keeping the joke clear.

03 / INSIDE THE PROJECT

What the experience includes.

  • A food-themed homepage and visual product showcase.
  • Humorous headings such as 'The TIMMY Blueprint' and 'Asset Portfolio'.
  • Share-card metadata and mobile-friendly presentation.

04 / IMPLEMENTATION

What powers it.

A static HTML and Tailwind-styled page with hosted food imagery and an explicit GitHub Pages address in its metadata.

05 / MY CONTRIBUTION

My role.

Creative direction, concept writing and playful web implementation.
