DISCIPLINE

Fashion and brand experience

01 / THE STARTING POINT

Why I created it.

A fashion house serves different audiences: clients looking for bespoke clothing and learners seeking skills. Its digital presence needs to guide both clearly.

02 / THE DIRECTION

The design approach.

Separate the atelier's fashion work from its educational offering while keeping one distinctive brand identity.

03 / INSIDE THE PROJECT

What the experience includes.

  • Bespoke fashion and bridalwear presentation.
  • Fashion academy and training information.
  • About, services, journal, contact and policy pages.
  • Structured page data and navigation across multiple destinations.

04 / IMPLEMENTATION

What powers it.

A multi-page HTML, CSS and JavaScript site with shared application code and content files. Its repository includes testing artifacts and a range of fashion brand imagery.

05 / MY CONTRIBUTION

My role.

Website design, page structure and brand presentation.
