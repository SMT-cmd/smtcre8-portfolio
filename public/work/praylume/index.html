Praylume.

A faith-centered application for prayer, scripture, personal reflection and spiritual community.

THE PRODUCT TODAY

Application in development

The project has actual prayer, scripture, journaling and scheduling routes plus official app icons. Public access and each community workflow should be confirmed before describing it as a broadly available release.

DISCIPLINE

Faith & community

Built around practical needs, with ongoing refinements where needed.

01 / THE STARTING POINT

Why I started it.

Prayer habits, scripture study, personal notes and community encouragement often happen in separate places. Praylume explores what it feels like when those practices can live together without becoming distracting.

02 / THE IDEA

How the solution took shape.

A calm personal space first, then appropriate connection to others. Private reflection and shared spiritual experiences should remain distinct so users understand what stays personal.

03 / THE EXPERIENCE

What the product includes.

  • Personal prayer sessions with duration-based entry points.
  • Bible-reading space and a reflective journal.
  • Prayer schedules and reminders for building a routine.
  • Installable app identity and navigation designed around faith practice.

04 / THE BUILD

How it works.

The application repository contains its own routes, PWA manifest and branded graphics. Development of broader faith-community and administrative experiences is separate from the features already represented in the code.

05 / MY CONTRIBUTION

The work behind it.

Original concept, workflow planning, product identity, application architecture and development direction.
